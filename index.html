Who We Are

Engineering clean water, together.

WATreat is a new University of Waterloo student design team focused on developing practical and innovative solutions for contaminated water treatment. Our multidisciplinary team brings together students from engineering, science, business, and design to research, build, and test a complete water treatment system for emergency scenarios.

By competing in the Canadian National Water Treatment Competition, our members gain hands-on experience in technical design, prototyping, project management, budgeting, and teamwork while addressing real-world water challenges. Our goal is to help advance accessible and sustainable solutions that contribute to clean drinking water for all.

Partner With WATreat!

Support from sponsors allows WATreat to purchase materials, build better prototypes, conduct testing, and represent the University of Waterloo at the Canadian National Water Treatment Competitions.

By partnering with WATreat, organizations can support student innovation, connect with emerging engineering talent, and contribute to the development of sustainable water treatment solutions. Explore our sponsorship package to learn more about partnership opportunities and sponsor benefits.
